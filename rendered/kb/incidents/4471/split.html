Fixing the parser will not close #4471. The hint and one-day reports stay open until their own fixes ship.

The three problems

  • parser (#1): the shared parser reads dates month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. Day and month swap: 7 April shows as 4 July.
  • hint (#2): the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type the month first as told, and the form reads the date day first. These are form reports from booking-v3 tenants only, and they need a form fix, not a parser fix.
  • one-day (#3): the date moves by one day and is not swapped (“Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.”). The desk had already noted these in desk-notes.json. That looks like a time zone conversion, so it needs a fix of its own.

How the reports were counted

  • One id, one report. The export repeats some rows because its pages overlap, so each report is counted once.
  • By tenant, not by intake tag. The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts). The table shows how often the tag was wrong.
  • By what the report says. The one-day moves are the only reports with no swapped day and month. The desk’s notes flag them, and reading every report body confirms it.

The counts come from src/lib/kb/split.ts, which runs at render time. When the desk merges more reports into #4471, the table updates.

For the desk

  • Matching on subject merged three bugs into one ticket. Decision 0001 stays as it is, because accepted decisions are never edited, but it needs a new record that dedupes by cause.
